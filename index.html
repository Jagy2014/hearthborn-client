    Hearthborn Client¶

    A custom client for Haven & Hearth

    Plan your village. Simplify production. Explore with better tools.

    Features · Documentation · Privacy · Support development · Screenshots

    Built on top of the original (vanilla) Haven & Hearth client, Hearthborn is an independent, unofficial custom client focused on improving everyday gameplay through a customizable interface and tools for village management, production, exploration, mining, and more.

    Languages: English and Russian (approximately 98% of the client translated into Russian) · Technology: Java 22 / Apache Ant

    Features¶

    Explore each feature in its own illustrated guide.

    🎛️ Interface & controls¶

    • Redesigned interface — Classic 4×4 or sidebar menu, movable toolbars, snapping windows, mirrored HUD, and compact mode.
    • Redesigned Action Search — Favorites, history, recipe details, and ingredient search.

    ⚙️ Production & management¶

    • Quality Roster — Record object quality and display known values on your plans.
    • Cattle Roster — Redesigned livestock overview with automatic removal of slaughtered animals.
    • Village & Claim Planner — Automatically map observed structures, name areas, measure tiles, and export or import plans.
    • Cheese Production Assistant — Cheese aging stages, rack labels, and progression chart.
    • Automatic Timers — Production, cheese, siege, claim, and manual timers.
    • Storage & Production Visibility — Cupboard contents, stockpile identification, and object labels.
    • Automation & Xtensions — Helpers for fuel, workstations, crops, animals, and other repetitive tasks.

    🗺️ Navigation & exploration¶

    • Improved Pathfinding — On by default, with optional horseback navigation.
    • Mapping & Exports — Transfer maps between clients or export images and tiles.
    • Session Trail — View your route for the current session without recording it.
    • Map Icons & Display Settings — More icons, animal sounds, and redesigned map controls.
    • Meteorite Triangulation — Narrow a meteorite search area using Star Gaze observations.

    ⛏️ Mining & combat¶

    • Reimagined Minesweeper — Mark cave tiles, inspect dust clues, and manage mining safety options.
    • Combat Assistance — Animal auto-combat and a compact combat interface.

    Getting started¶

    Hearthborn is an unofficial custom client for Haven & Hearth. Installation and launcher instructions will be linked here when a verified distribution is available.

    To explore the available features and controls, start with the interface guide and automation guide.

    Documentation¶

    Detailed guides with screenshots are available throughout the documentation. Start with the interface guide or storage and stockpiles, or use the feature links above to open individual guides.

    Note: Timers, quality information, maps, and automation rely on data actually observed by the client. They do not reveal unexplored information or guarantee successful actions.

    Privacy¶

    Hearthborn does not send your data to third-party services. It only sends the data required to connect to and play on the official Haven & Hearth servers.

    Support development¶

    Hearthborn is free for everyone. Donations are voluntary and help support continued development, bug fixes, and new features. They do not unlock exclusive features or provide in-game advantages.
