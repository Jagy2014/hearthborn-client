# Village and Claim Plans

The **Village Plan** in Hearthborn is an automatically generated overview of your settlement. As you explore, the client records observed structures and terrain so you can plan construction, organize production, and find important objects without drawing a map by hand.

The plan can include:

- **Claim boundaries**, including areas extended by claim flags.
- **Fences, paths, fields, and buildings.**
- **Trees and production objects**, including furnaces and smelters.
- **Village infrastructure**, such as the village idol, chicken coops, and rabbit hutches.
- **Leatherworking equipment**, including tanning tubs and drying racks.

You can also add your own named areas and rename supported objects to make the plan easier to navigate.

## Automatic mapping

The plan builds up as the client observes the settlement. You do not need to place each fence, building, tree, field, road, or production object manually.

Claim borders are included, along with extensions created using claim flags. As you visit more of the settlement, the saved plan becomes more complete.

**Important:** The plan reflects terrain, claim information, and objects the client has observed. It does not reveal unexplored areas or guarantee that every saved object is still present or unchanged.

## Add and name an area

Use named areas to label parts of the settlement that have a specific purpose, such as **Leather Storage**, **Wood**, **Metal**, or **Silk/Cloth**.

1. Open the Village Plan.
2. Choose **Add area**.
3. Mark the area you want to identify.
4. Give it a descriptive name.

Named areas help distinguish storage and production zones even when the underlying objects look similar.

## Rename a building or object

1. Open the Village Plan.
2. Select the building or supported object on the plan or from the object list.
3. Choose **Rename object** and enter a name.

For houses, the name is also displayed above the building in the game world. This makes it easier to identify workshops and storage houses without opening the plan each time.

## Quality information

Hearthborn connects the plan with information already recorded in the **Quality Journal**:

- **Trees:** show their recorded quality on the plan.
- **Furnaces and smelters:** show their recorded quality when available.

The plan does not invent or measure quality values on its own. If an object's quality is not in the journal, there may be no quality value to display.

## Personal and village plans

Hearthborn automatically builds plans for both **personal claims** and **villages**, using the same mapping and object-recording features.

- **Personal claims:** You can create a plan for a claim you own. The client cannot grant plan access to other characters who merely have in-game permissions on that claim, because the server does not provide the ownership and permission details needed to distinguish those access levels. For plan access, the client can only distinguish **owner** from **non-owner**.
- **Village claims:** All village members can create a plan of their village. The plan fills in as members explore and observe its structures and terrain.

These restrictions concern access to plan creation, not a change to the game's underlying claim permissions.

## Measure distances and areas

Select **Measure tiles** to measure a rectangular section of the plan in tiles. This is useful for checking the dimensions of a field, planning a building, or estimating the space available for a production area before construction.

## Navigate and manage plans

- Switch between **Personal claim** and **Village claim** views.
- Use the searchable **Object list** to locate an observed object and center the plan on it.
- Rename and manage saved plans.
- Use **Measure tiles** to check dimensions on the map.

## Export, share, and import

Plans can be exported in several ways:

- **Excel:** Export the plan for viewing and working with its information in a spreadsheet.
- **HTML:** Export a version that can be opened in a web browser.
- **Plan file:** Export the plan's data and send the file to another Hearthborn client. The recipient can **import** it and continue using the transferred plan in their client.

Excel and HTML are viewing/export formats; use the plan-file export and import workflow when you want another player to load and use the plan inside Hearthborn.


## Notes and limitations

- The plan is based on **observed and saved data**, not a live view of the entire world.
- Some details may need to be observed again after the settlement changes.
- Displayed quality values depend on entries in the **Quality Journal**.
- The server does not expose the detailed personal-claim permissions required to give plan-creation access to permitted non-owners. Only the owner can create a personal-claim plan.
- All members of a village can create its village plan.
- Exporting or importing a plan does not change in-game claim ownership or permissions.
