# Production tools and timers

## Cheese production assistant

Follow cheese progression for cow, sheep, and goat milk, inspect aging stages and expected products, and see labels on racks. Observed batches appear in the **Cheese** tab of the Timers window.

![Cheese aging stages](../images/cheese-1.jpg)

The progression chart helps compare expected products and choose aging stages and locations.

![Cheese progression chart](../images/cheese-2.jpg)

## Automatic timers

Hearthborn starts timers for supported production processes as they are observed and notifies you when they are ready.

- **Ore smelters, kilns, ovens, and finery forges:** Processing countdowns and completion notices.
- **Steel crucibles:** Observed fuel countdowns and refill reminders.
- **Cheese:** Aging-stage tracking.
- **Silk and herbalist tables:** Supported growth and processing stages.
- **Battering rams and siege equipment:** Preparation countdowns.
- **Personal claims:** Activation countdown after placement.

The Timers window contains **Automatic**, **Cheese**, and **Manual** tabs; you can also create your own timers.

![Automatic production timers](<../images/automatic timers.jpg>)

**Limitations:** Timers use information last observed by the client. A process that changes while unobserved may have an outdated estimate. Saved deadlines continue counting down after a restart.

## Storage and production visibility

- Show observed cupboard contents as item icons above cupboards. You can **change the displayed icon from the cupboard's menu**.
- Show **ore stockpiles with icons** indicating their contents. **Stone, brick, and clay stockpiles use distinct colors** to make materials easier to distinguish.
- **Hold Shift** to display stockpile names.
- Show cheese rack labels and selected object radii.
