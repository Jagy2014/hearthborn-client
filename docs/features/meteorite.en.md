# Meteorite triangulation

When a **falling-star notification** appears, the **Star Gaze** action becomes available. Use it to determine the direction of the expected meteorite impact.

## How triangulation works

Each time you use **Star Gaze**, Hearthborn draws the indicated direction on the map from the location where you performed the action. Repeat **Star Gaze** from different locations to add more direction lines. Their intersection helps you narrow down the likely meteorite landing area.

The map shows your observations while the starfall event is active, allowing you to compare the directions and plan your search. You can also follow the server-provided starfall forecast.

## Managing observation lines

- **While the event is active:** Star Gaze direction lines remain visible on the map.
- **Remove an observation manually:** **Right-click (RMB)** the point on the map where you used **Star Gaze** to remove its line.
- **After the meteorite falls:** The **Star Gaze** action disappears, and the observation lines are automatically removed from the map.

*Triangulation helps estimate the landing area; it does not reveal the exact impact location.*
